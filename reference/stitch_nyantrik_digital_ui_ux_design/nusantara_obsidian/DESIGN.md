## Brand & Style

This design system blends Indonesia's national cultural heritage with a contemporary, high-contrast digital innovation aesthetic inspired by modern governmental and technological initiatives. Rooted in deep cultural resonance and clean statecraft, it transitions from soft nocturnal tones to an uncompromising, ultra-crisp dark canvas. 

### Visual Archetype & Style
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Deep Charcoal Innovation (High-Contrast Editorial Dark Mode meets Dynamic Indonesian Cultural Futurism).
- **Spatial Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian and warm charcoal backdrops replace traditional blue-indigo tints, letting cultural accents pop like illuminated museum artifacts in a state-of-the-art auditorium.
- **Graphic Accents:** Clean geometric cards with subtle borders, enriched by energetic golden cultural highlights and celebratory prismatic gradients.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, inspiring, technologically capable, and forward-looking, uniting traditional artisans and digital innovators alike.

## Colors

The color palette centers around an absolute dark bedrock, stark pure whites, and vivid cultural accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - `surface-charcoal` (`#121212`): The foundational deep obsidian canvas providing maximum contrast and visual focus.
  - `surface-container` (`#1C1D21`): Primary card and modular container background.
  - `surface-container-elevated` (`#222328`): Interactive hover fills, active modal cards, and floating panels.
  - `outline-subtle` (`#2E3038`): A crisp, low-opacity boundary defining containers against deep backgrounds without heavy visual noise.
- **Primary & Action Accents:**
  - `gold-action` (`#F4B400` / `#EAA510`): High-energy primary CTA gold symbolizing cultural mastery and vitality.
  - `soga-copper` (`#D49A5B`): Earthy soga textile pigment for secondary highlights, awards, and regional markers.
  - `nusantara-teal` (`#34B9B0`): Vibrant cyan-teal accent for digital innovation metrics, progress states, and tags.
- **Foreground & Text Contrast:**
  - `text-primary` (`#FFFFFF`): Pure crisp white for headlines, primary buttons, and critical values.
  - `text-secondary` (`#A1A1AA`): Balanced light gray delivering readable, comfortable body copy on dark surfaces.
  - `text-muted` (`#71717A`): Subdued tone for timestamps, legal disclaimers, and metadata.

## Typography

The typography leverages a unified, contemporary grotesque typeface throughout the interface. By utilizing heavy headline weights with geometric precision, the brand strikes an authoritative and tech-driven balance, celebrating modern Indonesian progress while retaining warmth.

- **Scale & Contrast:** Display and headline variants feature dense tracking and tight leading to deliver punchy, bold visual declarations. Body styles provide generous line heights to ensure long-form cultural proposals and competition technical briefs are effortlessly scannable.
- **Labels & Metas:** Navigation labels, badge chips, and category indicators leverage uppercase transformations and expanded letter spacing for crisp clarity against charcoal backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column responsive fluid grid aligns complex content, multi-tier prize listings, and timeline processes.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout, max-width `1280px`, `3rem` canvas margins, and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` margin, and `1rem` column gutters.
- **Rhythm & Gaps:** All spatial separation strictly adheres to the defined space scale. Internal card padding uses `space-lg`, form inputs utilize `space-md`, and structural segment intervals rely on `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through low-contrast contours and subtle translucent surfaces rather than heavy blur shadows, maintaining a pristine, architectural presence.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Base background `#121212`.
- **Layer 1 (Containers & Cards):** `#1C1D21` paired with a clean `1px solid #2E3038` perimeter outline.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated & Popups):** `#222328` paired with a diffused ambient shadow (`0 12px 36px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`).
- **Cultural Accent Highlights:** Distinctive hero cards or active states employ an ambient gold rim glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(244, 180, 0, 0.18)`), echoing incandescent lighting over exhibition artifacts.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a rounded form factor (Level 2). This creates approachable, modern edges that contrast softly against the stark charcoal backdrop.

- **Pill / Fully-Rounded:** Applied to high-impact primary buttons (e.g., "DAFTAR SEKARANG", "UNDUH") to mirror the iconic action pills of the identity.
- **Default Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Form factors for competition tracks, criteria cards, and criteria timelines.
- **Form Controls & Badges (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Applied to form fields, filter chips, and subcategory pill indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Golden CTA):** Solid `#F4B400` background, `#121212` text, heavy bold weight (`700`), fully rounded pill shape (`9999px`). Includes a directional arrow icon. On hover, shifts to `#EAA510` with subtle elevation.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, `1px solid #2E3038` border, `#FFFFFF` text. On hover, transitions border to `#F4B400` and background to `rgba(244, 180, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Download Action Button:** Compact pill with vibrant `#F4B400` fill and dark typography, featuring a document or download indicator.

### Cards & Track Containers
- **Content Cards:** Surface fill `#1C1D21`, enclosed in `1px solid #2E3038`.
- **Category Badge:** Positioned at the top of cards, styled in uppercase tracking (`#A1A1AA` or `#F4B400` in `label-sm`).
- **Prize & Highlight Cards:** Employs vibrant background gradients (such as golden radial gradients for top honors, copper gradients for runners-up) paired with crisp black typography and illustrated medals.

### Lists & Accordions
- **Timeline Lists:** Clean horizontal or vertical chains. Step numbers highlighted in `#34B9B0` or `#F4B400`, accompanied by white title headings and `#A1A1AA` dates.
- **FAQ Accordions:** Low-profile container rows with hairline bottom borders (`#2E3038`), expanding smoothly with `#F4B400` toggle glyphs.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Surface background `#121212` nested within `#1C1D21` cards. Border `1px solid #2E3038`.
- Focused state applies a sharp `#F4B400` outline with zero fuzzy spread, ensuring technical precision.
- Placeholder text set to `#71717A`.

### Badges & Chips
- **Category Chips:** Filled with `rgba(46, 48, 56, 0.6)`, text `#A1A1AA`, hoverable state shifting text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Status Badges:** Small compact pills displaying `#34B9B0` (Active/Open) or `#F4B400` (Featured).
